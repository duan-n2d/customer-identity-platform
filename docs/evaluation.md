## Method

* **Precision** is pairwise: of the record pairs placed in one cluster, the share that really are the same person. A false merge shows a customer another customer's data, so precision is the metric the default profile optimises.
* **Linkable recall.** Two records of one person can only be linked if they share some evidence (phone, email, date of birth). In this data most true pairs share nothing, and no algorithm can find them; recall over *all* true pairs mostly measures the data. We report recall over the linkable pairs, and the linkable share separately.
